Regional Health Authority Consultants Distribution

Exploring the distribution of medical consultants across RHAs

Consultant Distribution by Specialty at Regional Level

This interactive visualization shows how medical consultants were distributed across different specialties in the Regional Health Authorities following the 1974 NHS reorganization. The data provides important insights into workforce planning and resource allocation at the regional level.

Consultants by Specialty (RHA)

Regional Workforce Planning

The visualization above presents the distribution of consultants across different medical specialties for each Regional Health Authority. This data reveals broader patterns in how specialized medical expertise was allocated across larger geographic regions.

Key Observations

  • Regional variations in specialty distribution reflect different healthcare priorities and needs
  • Some regions show concentration in specific specialties, suggesting centers of excellence
  • Regional workforce planning shows patterns of specialization and resource allocation

Comparative Analysis

When examined alongside the Area Health Authority data, these regional visualizations provide valuable insights into how the NHS implemented its tiered administrative structure for healthcare planning and resource allocation.
